# Introduction

As AI adoption grows across organizations, individual agents that handle a broad range of tasks can become difficult to maintain and prone to routing errors. Multi-agent architectures offer a way to address this by distributing responsibilities across specialized agents that collaborate to handle complex, cross-domain workloads.

## Scenario

Consider a scenario at Fabrikam, Inc., where the enterprise support team built a single Copilot Studio agent to handle all customer interactions. Over time, the agent grew to include more than 40 actions spanning billing, technical support, and account management, each owned by a different team. As the action count climbed, routing accuracy degraded and updates from one team risked breaking functionality owned by another. The team now needs to rethink the architecture before moving forward.

## What you'll learn

This module uses the fictional Fabrikam, Inc. scenario to illustrate the concepts and decisions involved in designing a multi-agent solution. You'll explore what multi-agent architectures are, why they're useful, and when they make sense compared to a single-agent approach. You'll compare orchestration patterns, examine the distinction between child agents and connected agents, and learn how to connect your agent to agents built on various platforms.

## Goal

By the end of this module, you'll be able to design and build a multi-agent solution in Copilot Studio.
