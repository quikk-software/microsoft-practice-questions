# Summary and resources

Microsoft Dataverse is more than a database—it's the intelligent data foundation that makes Power Platform solutions secure, scalable, and AI-ready.

In this module, you saw how Dataverse eliminates the complexity of traditional databases by combining built-in security, the Common Data Model, and low-code development into a single platform. You learned how to structure business data with tables, columns, and relationships, and how to enforce data policies through business rules, Power Fx, and Power Automate—without writing database code.

You also discovered how forms and views put the right data in front of the right users in model-driven apps, and how Microsoft Copilot lets makers build entire data models through natural language—no technical expertise required.

Most importantly, you saw how Dataverse powers the next generation of AI experiences. When your business data lives in Dataverse, it becomes grounded knowledge for AI agents built in Copilot Studio. The Dataverse Model Context Protocol (MCP) server makes that data queryable and actionable in real time—turning your structured records into the fuel for intelligent, automated business processes.

After completing this module, you're able to:

*   Explain how Microsoft Dataverse differs from traditional databases and why those differences matter for Power Platform solutions.
*   Identify and describe the structural components of Dataverse, including tables, columns, rows, and relationships.
*   Select the appropriate business logic mechanism—business rules, Power Fx, or Power Automate—for common data scenarios.
*   Describe how forms and views control data presentation in model-driven apps.
*   Use Microsoft Copilot to create and refine Dataverse tables, columns, and relationships through natural language conversations.
*   Describe how Dataverse supports AI and agent experiences through the Dataverse MCP server.
