# Describe the value of copilots and generative AI features in Microsoft Power Platform

Artificial intelligence has become a practical, everyday capability embedded throughout Power Platform—not a distant feature or an add-on. Whether you're building an app, creating a flow, designing a website, or analyzing data, AI is available to assist you at every step.

## What is generative AI?

Generative AI refers to AI systems that can create new content—text, data models, code, images, and more—based on patterns learned from large datasets. Unlike traditional software that follows a fixed set of rules, generative AI understands intent expressed in natural language and produces output tailored to that intent.

In practical terms, this means you can describe what you want in your own words, and the AI generates a meaningful starting point. You then refine that output until it matches your needs exactly. This iterative, conversational approach reduces the time and expertise needed to build solutions.

## Copilot across the Power Platform

Microsoft has embedded Copilot—an AI assistant powered by large language models—throughout the Power Platform. Each Copilot is context-aware, meaning it understands which tool you're working in and provides assistance specific to that context.

[![Screenshot of Power Automate workflow editor with Copilot assistance.](media/power-automate-copilot-pane-workflow-editor.png)](media/power-automate-copilot-pane-workflow-editor.png#lightbox)

*   **Copilot in Power Apps** helps you build apps by generating Dataverse tables, app screens, and navigation from a natural language description of your business need. You describe the app you want to build, and Copilot creates a working starting point.
*   **Copilot in Power Automate** helps you create flows by generating the steps, connectors, and logic from a description of the process you want to automate. It also helps you understand existing flows by explaining what each step does in plain language.
*   **Copilot in Power Pages** helps you build websites by generating page layouts, forms, and site text. Describe a page requirement and Copilot generates the content and the corresponding Dataverse tables.
*   **Copilot in Power BI** helps you create reports and dashboards by generating visualizations from descriptions of what you want to analyze. You can ask questions of your data in natural language and receive answers in the form of charts and summaries.
*   **Copilot in Dataverse** helps you design data models by suggesting tables, columns, and relationships based on your business scenario. Import data from Excel or SharePoint and Copilot proposes a data structure to match.

## AI Builder: Adding AI to your solutions

AI Builder is a capability within Power Platform that allows you to add pre-built and custom AI models to your apps and flows without writing code. Common uses include:

*   Extracting information from documents using document processing models.
*   Detecting objects in images.
*   Classifying text.
*   Predicting outcomes based on historical data.

For example, an accounts payable team might use an AI Builder document processing model to automatically extract vendor name, invoice number, line items, and total amount from scanned PDF invoices—eliminating manual data entry entirely.

## Responsible AI in Power Platform

Microsoft is committed to developing AI that is fair, reliable, safe, private, inclusive, transparent, and accountable. Power Platform's AI features include content filtering, transparency indicators, and human-in-the-loop design patterns that keep users informed and in control of AI-generated outputs. Copilot always presents its suggestions as starting points for human review, not final decisions.

Note

Copilot features in Power Platform require specific licensing and may be subject to regional availability. Check Microsoft's documentation for the latest licensing and availability information.
