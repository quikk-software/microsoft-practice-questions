# Summary

In this module, you learned how to take a canvas app from finished to live. You published a new version, explored how AutoSave works and when to save manually, and learned how users are notified when an update is ready. You shared the app with users and co-owners, set permissions using the new Share dialog, and learned when to use security groups instead of sharing with individuals. You also saw how coauthoring lets multiple makers edit an app simultaneously.

On the maintenance side, you learned how to browse version history, restore a previous version, and what to expect when working with older apps. Finally, you explored application lifecycle management — the ALM cycle, and the Power Platform tools that support it: Solutions for packaging and moving apps across environments, and Pipelines for deploying them with just a few clicks.
