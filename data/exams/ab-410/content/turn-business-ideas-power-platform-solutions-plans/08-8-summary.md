# Summary

Contoso started with a familiar problem—time-off requests buried in email, approvals tracked in spreadsheets, and HR going with the flow on company-wide PTO. Instead of spending weeks designing a solution from scratch, they used plans in Power Apps to describe the problem in plain language and let AI agents handle the solution design.

You followed that journey from start to finish. You learned how the three AI agents—the Requirement Agent, Data Agent, and Solution Agent—work together to transform a business problem into user requirements, a data model, and a technology proposal. You practiced writing effective prompts that give the agents enough context to produce useful output, and you explored how to refine each agent's results through inline editing and Copilot feedback. You then saw how to build each solution component directly from the plan, with each designer prefilled with context from your work. Finally, you learned the reverse workflow—creating a plan from an existing solution to document and understand what's already built.

These skills let you go from a business idea to a structured Power Platform solution in a fraction of the time it would take to design everything manually.
