# Introduction

Every business has processes that are overdue for modernization—approvals stuck in email threads, data scattered across spreadsheets, and manual handoffs that slow teams down. Microsoft Power Platform gives you a rich set of tools to solve these problems, but knowing where to start can feel overwhelming when you're staring at a blank canvas.

Plans in Power Apps changes that starting point. Instead of opening an app designer and building from scratch, you describe your business problem in plain language and let AI agents do the heavy lifting. Plans analyzes your description, identifies the user roles involved, recommends a data model, and proposes the right combination of apps, flows, and agents, all before you write a single formula or configure a single screen.

Contoso, a mid-size professional services firm, is facing exactly this challenge. Their employees submit time-off requests by email, managers approve them in a spreadsheet, and HR has no visibility into company-wide paid time off (PTO) balances. The process is slow, error-prone, and frustrating for everyone involved. Contoso's team decides to use Plans to turn this problem into a working Power Platform solution.

In this module, you explore Plans and the AI agents that power the experience. You learn how to write an effective prompt, refine the agent outputs for requirements, data, and technology proposals, and then build the solution components. You also learn how to create a plan from an existing solution to document and improve what you already have.

By the end of this module, you're able to use Plans to go from a business problem description to a complete Power Platform solution design.
