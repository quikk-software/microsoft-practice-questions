# Summary

In this module, you learned how to create and use AI Builder grounded prompts in Copilot Studio, Power Apps, and Power Automate. You learned how grounded prompts can help you generate natural language responses based on your data and business logic. You also learned how to train, test, and publish your prompts and how to give feedback to help improve them.
