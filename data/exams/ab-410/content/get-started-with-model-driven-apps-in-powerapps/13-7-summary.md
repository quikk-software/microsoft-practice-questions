# Summary

Congratulations on completing this module on model-driven apps!

Defining and enforcing consistent business processes is a key aspect of model-driven app design. Consistent processes help ensure that your app users can focus on their work and not have to remember to perform a set of manual steps.

Let's review what you've learned:

*   Model-driven app design is based on your data. This approach focuses on quickly adding components including dashboards, forms, views, and charts.
*   How security roles control access to data and how to share model-driven apps with users.
*   Different ways to incorporate business logic, including business process flows.
*   How to use Power Fx to create custom commands with logic and user notifications.
*   How to use generative pages — AI-generated React-based pages built from natural language descriptions.
*   How to enable and configure Copilot chat for app users.
*   How to add autonomous agents and app assistant agents to your model-driven app.
*   Little or no code is required to build model-driven apps.
