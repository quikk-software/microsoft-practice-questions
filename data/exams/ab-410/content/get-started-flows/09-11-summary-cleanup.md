# Summary

Let’s quickly review what you covered in this module.

In this module, you learned the basics of Power Automate, including the difference between triggers and actions and how to create flows for yourself or your team. You created a flow that automatically saves email attachments, built scheduled flows, and even troubleshot them.

## Next steps

Now that you're introduced to **Power Automate**, take a look at a few common scenarios. Some of the most frequently used flows include approvals and templates.

Approvals help automate request and approval processes, including notifications and tracking responses. Additionally, **Power Automate** provides hundreds of templates that you can use as a starting point and customize as needed.

To learn more, see [Build approval flows with Power Automate](/en-us/training/modules/build-approval-flows/?azure-portal=true) and [Use Power Automate to increase productivity](/en-us/training/modules/use-power-automate-increase-productivity/?azure-portal=true).
